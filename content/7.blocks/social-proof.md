Logos are simple wordmarks made from an icon and a name, so they follow your theme in light and dark mode. Pass your own list through the `logos` prop, or swap each wordmark for an `img` or inline SVG of the real logo. Marquees use a small scoped keyframe animation, so they work when copied on their own.
