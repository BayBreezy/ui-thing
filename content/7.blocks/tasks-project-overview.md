These blocks give a bird's eye view of projects. They take typed props (`project`, `projects`, `people`, `milestones` or `initial`) with fictional sample data. Sorting, filtering, searching, favorites and the week toggle all run on the client.

Progress is always shown with a progress element or a circular progress that has an accessible name, and the workload bars are labelled with the numbers they stand for. Status colors never carry meaning alone, because the status is also written as text.

## Style One

Project header with a status badge, team avatars, quick actions, four stat cards for total, completed, in progress and overdue tasks and an overall progress bar.
