Checklists work because they show progress and one clear next step. These blocks keep their tasks in a local copy of a typed prop (`items` or `milestones`), so ticking something never mutates the data you pass in. Connect each action to your own app, and store the result on the user so the checklist remembers where they left off.

Tasks and names are fictional. Every progress indicator has an accessible name and counts are announced through live regions. Dismiss and close buttons always have a way to bring the content back in these demos, so you can preview both states.
