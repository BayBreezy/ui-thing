---
title: Banner
description: Banners for announcements, promotions, alerts, consent and release notes. Dismissible with an animated collapse, with live countdowns, validated forms and working states.
---

Every banner here can be dismissed with an animated exit. Previews also show a **Show banner again** button so you can replay them. Remove that button, and the `visible` state if you want to control it from outside, when you copy a banner into your app. The floating banners (cookie consent and release notes) use `fixed` positioning.
