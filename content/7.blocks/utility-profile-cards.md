Use these blocks wherever a person needs to be shown: a social profile, a team page, a comment thread or an address book. They take typed props with fictional people, such as `user`, `members`, `profile`, `contact` and `followers`, with portraits from Pravatar and Unsplash.

Nothing is sent anywhere. Follow buttons and tabs keep their state locally, so connect them to your own API.

## Style One

User card with a gradient banner, an overlapping avatar, a verified badge, bio, follower stats and a follow button that updates the follower count.
