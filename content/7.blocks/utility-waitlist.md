These blocks help you collect interest before a product opens. Each one validates the email address inline and shows a confirmation, so they work on their own as a landing page or as a section inside a larger page. They take typed props with fictional data, such as the `title`, `features`, `tiers` and `milestones` objects.

Nothing is sent anywhere. Submitting only changes the local state of the block, so connect the form to your own mailing list or API.

## Style One

Coming soon page with a launch badge, a short feature list and an email field. Submitting replaces the form with a confirmation that repeats the address.
