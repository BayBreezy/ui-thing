These blocks help people choose. They take typed props for the plans and features, so you can swap in your own rows. Checks and dashes always come with text for screen readers, so nothing is shown with color or an icon alone.

Switches, selection and expand buttons only change what is shown. Selecting a plan shows a toast, so connect those buttons to your checkout or your sales form.
