Show photos, projects and results with these blocks. They take typed props with fictional content, such as `photos`, `projects`, `studies`, `videos` and `items`, using Unsplash photos that are already used elsewhere in the library. Images are lazy loaded and every one has alt text.

Nothing is uploaded or fetched beyond the images. The video block uses a demo clock in place of a real player, so swap in your own `<video>` element or embed. Motion respects the reduced motion setting.

## Style One

Masonry gallery with mixed aspect ratios, category filter chips with a live count, and captions that appear on hover and on keyboard focus.
