These blocks are for the public side of your integrations: a page that shows which tools work with your product. They take typed props with a list of tools, and the logos come from the Iconify `logos` set, so you can swap them for your own images.

Search, tabs, connect buttons and votes only change local state. The connect and disconnect buttons are for show, so replace them with your OAuth flow. If you want the settings screen where a signed in user manages their integrations, see the integrations block in the settings blocks.
