Every simple form validates the email, shows an inline error, simulates a request and swaps to a confirmation with an Undo button. Replace the `submit` function with your own API call. Style Five uses `vee-validate` with a yup schema, and Style Ten is a two step flow.

Split layout with the copy and form on one side and a CSS email mockup of the latest issue on the other. Validated email field with loading and success states.
