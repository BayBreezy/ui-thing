---
title: Footer
description: Footer layouts for marketing sites and apps. Link columns, validated newsletters, call to action panels, an accordion for mobile, a theme and language bar, contact cards and a sitemap, all using theme tokens so they work in dark mode.
---

Footers take a `name` prop and ship with realistic links, so replace the arrays with your own. Social icons use `currentColor` so they follow your theme. The newsletter forms validate the email and show a success state; connect them to your provider.
