The last screen of an onboarding flow should confirm that it worked and point at one clear next move. These blocks do that without confetti, so they stay calm for people who prefer reduced motion, and the one animated check is skipped entirely when the system asks for less movement.

Workspace names, people and numbers are fictional and come in through typed props. Success messages use `role="status"` so screen readers announce them.

## Style One

All set screen with a check that springs in, a short message with the workspace name and two actions.
