::prose-callout{variant="warning" title="Not legal advice"}
The policy text in these blocks is placeholder content for a fictional company. It is not legal advice. Have a qualified lawyer review and replace it with your own terms before you publish.
::

The document blocks take a typed `sections` array with `id`, `title`, `summary`, `paragraphs` and an optional `list`, so you can paste in your own policy without touching the markup. Cookie and consent blocks keep choices in local state, so persist them yourself and load analytics only after consent.
